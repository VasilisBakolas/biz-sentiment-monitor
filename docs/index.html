Biz Sentiment Monitor

Reads the 4chan /biz/ board every three minutes, scores bullish and bearish language for BTC and ETH, and flags days more than two standard deviations from the previous two weeks. A contrarian gauge of retail mood, not a trading signal.

Why I built it. I wanted to know whether the chatter on /biz/ says anything about BTC and ETH prices, and I wanted to learn data analysis on real, messy data instead of a clean course dataset. After six years of reading and judging online content as a content moderator, text was the obvious place to start. It began out of curiosity, and I then tested it the way I would at work: hand-labelled posts, error rates and honest limits. The short answer is that it mostly does not predict prices, and the page shows how I got there.

Built by Vasileios Bakolas, Berlin, 2026. Data analyst moving into fintech and crypto. LinkedIn · GitHub

In short: over 127 days, /biz/ sentiment did not lead BTC or ETH prices. The more useful result was learning where the scoring itself goes wrong.

What I found

  • Over 127 days of collection (5 Jul to 8 Nov 2026), spikes in /biz/ sentiment did not lead BTC or ETH price moves. The window is short, so this says "no evidence yet", not "no relationship".
  • I checked the scoring against 137 posts I labelled by hand. When it calls a direction it is right about 7 times in 10, but it misses most posts that carry a clear mood, and it scores only 37% of stored posts.
  • The weak point is reading the language, not the pipeline. Better word matching helped precision but not coverage, so the next step is a fresh test set before adding more words.

Table and notes

    Images: reading the memes

    /biz/ is an image board, and much of its mood sits in the pictures, not the words. A pink wojak usually means despair, and a bull cartoon like Mumu usually means confidence. Since 8 Nov 2026 the scraper reads them too.

    1. 1 · Fetch

      For each post with an image, download the small preview the board already serves (about 125 px wide), one at a time and slowly.

    2. 2 · Match

      Compare it with 155 reference pictures I collected: 38 pink wojaks (bearish), 101 Mumu pictures and 16 green wojaks (bullish). A visual fingerprint survives resizing, recompression and mirroring.

    3. 3 · Store numbers

      Keep only a label (bullish, bearish or none), the share of green and red pixels, and the date. The picture itself is discarded and never published.

    • Status. Collecting since 8 Nov 2026. There are no results yet. Old images are gone from the board, so the history cannot be filled in, and the method needs the same hand-labelled check as the words before I trust it.
    • Plan. A daily image-mood index with z-scores against the previous 14 days, set beside the text sentiment and the BTC and ETH prices.
    • Known limits. It only recognises copies of the pictures I supplied, so a different pink wojak drawing is missed. Colour is stored but not used for the label, because a red chart can mean either mood. Sarcasm is not handled.
    How one poll works
      • Threads whose reply count has not grown are skipped, so each poll fetches only what changed and stays inside the API's rate limit.
      • The primary key is (post_id, ticker) with INSERT OR IGNORE, so polling the same thread twice never double counts.
      • Reports show distinct threads and posters beside raw post counts, because one user posting repeatedly inflates volume.
      • A post with no sentiment words gets None, not 0, so silence is not read as neutral.
      • The baseline uses the previous 14 days without the day being scored, and the sample standard deviation (n − 1).
      • Failed requests are caught inside the loop, and launchd restarts the process if it ever exits.

      What I learned
      
  
      
  
        
    
      • Check the scoring before trusting it. Against 137 posts I labelled by hand, the first scoring was right 61% of the time when it called a direction. Better word matching raised that to 71%.
        • 
    
      • Coverage matters as much as accuracy. The scoring reads a direction in only 37% of BTC and ETH posts, so a precise score on a third of the data is still a thin signal.
        • 
    
      • Silence is not neutral. A post with no sentiment words gets no score at all, instead of a zero, so quiet posts do not pull the average toward the middle.
        • 
    
      • Volume follows the market. ETH post volume rises with big price moves, but it does not predict them, which is why I checked the next-day link separately.
        • 
    
      • A short window proves little. 127 days can rule out a strong link but not a weak one, so the honest result is "no evidence yet", not "no relationship".
        • 
  
      

      


      
  
      
    
      What this does not show yet
      
  
      
  
        
    
      • Scoring in the snapshot. The data above was scored with the original substring matching, where "path" scores as "ath", "drug" as "rug", and "selling" counts as both "sell" and "selling". A whole-word version with negation is written and tested (see Evaluation) but is not yet applied to this snapshot.
        • 
    
      • Short price history. The price test covers 127 days in one market phase and only a handful of tops and bottoms. It can rule out a strong relationship but not a weak one. Forward windows also overlap, so the observations are less independent than the counts suggest. The next step is a longer price history.
        • 
    
      • Thin ETH data. ETH has a median of  matching posts a day and no sentiment reading on  of 127 days, so its averages are noisier than BTC's and some of its tops and bottoms have no reading at all.
        • 
    
      • Partial edge days. The first and last days of the snapshot are partial, because collection started and the export happened part way through those days.

      In progress
      
    
      This is a working project, not a finished product. Where it stands:
      
  
      
  
        
    
      • Done. Scraper running continuously since 5 Jul 2026, daily z-score flags, BTC and ETH price comparison, a first hand-labelled evaluation, and, since 8 Nov 2026, the whole-word scoring with negation and the image signal both running live.
        • 
    
      • Next. A fresh set of hand-labelled posts to test more words fairly. The new score is stored beside the old one for every new post, so the two can be compared on the same posts.
        • 
    
      • Also planned. Store full post text and a lexicon version, filter spam, and compare against a language-model classifier.
        • 
    
      • Images. /biz/ is an image board, so a second signal reads memes: it matches the small preview of each image against reference pictures of the pink wojak (bearish) and Mumu the bull (bullish). It has been collecting since 8 Nov 2026, stores only numbers and never the images, and it needs the same hand-labelled check before I report any result. History cannot be filled in, because old threads and images are gone.
